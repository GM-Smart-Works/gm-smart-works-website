What we do

Structural steel work, done right the first time.

Structural Steel

Steel Erection

On-site erection of structural steel frames, from warehouse and canopy structures to full industrial steelwork.

We coordinate lifting, alignment, bolting and final positioning for frames, portal structures and support steel.

  • Crane and rigging coordination
  • Connection and bolt-up installation
  • Site sequencing for safer builds

Installations

Installation of structural steel components, piping structures, and industrial steel fittings.

Our installation work covers brackets, platforms, pipe supports and steel fittings that need a precise fit on site.

  • Structural component placement
  • Piping and support steel installation
  • Fit-up checks before handover

Fabrications

Structural fabrications and reworking of existing steel installations to meet new site or operational requirements.

We fabricate and modify components to suit site changes, repairs, upgrades and new load requirements.

  • Workshop and site-based modification
  • Repairs and strengthening work
  • Custom steel parts for project needs

Consulting

Project Consulting

On-site consulting and advisory services for structural steel projects, including planning, design review, and installation guidance.

We support planning, scope checking and practical buildability reviews before steel work starts.

  • Scope and method guidance
  • Build sequence planning
  • Practical site advice for teams

Site Supervision

On-site supervision and oversight of steel installation projects to ensure quality and compliance.

We monitor installation progress, quality checks and the handoff between teams so the work stays controlled.

  • Quality and progress oversight
  • Safety and compliance checks
  • Client feedback during execution
Trusted materials

How we source and verify steel supplies

Certified stockists

We source through established steel stockists and processors who can provide traceability, batch consistency and the right product grades for the job.

Project-specific selection

Material choices are matched to span, load, exposure and corrosion requirements instead of relying on one-size-fits-all stock.

Quality checks

Incoming material is checked before fabrication or installation so we can catch specification issues early.

Supply coordination

We plan material purchases around the build schedule so fabrication, delivery and site work stay aligned and projects avoid avoidable delays.

Why choose us

A team that shows up, and finishes the job.

GM Smart Works has been registered and operating since 2018, with hands-on experience erecting structural steel frames for warehouses, canopies, and industrial sites, plus installation and fabrication work on plant piping and structural fittings. Our team has the skills and knowledge to handle complex steelwork projects, ensuring that every job is completed safely, efficiently, and to the highest standards.

We use only high-quality steel and materials from trusted suppliers, ensuring that every project meets industry standards and client expectations. Our team is committed to precision and attention to detail, delivering durable and reliable steel structures.

We pride ourselves on our reliability and professionalism. From initial consultation to project completion, we maintain clear communication with our clients, ensuring that every project is completed on time and to the highest standards.
